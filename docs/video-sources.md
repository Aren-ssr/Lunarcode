## Before charging for access

Publicly viewable YouTube videos are not automatically licensed for every commercial use. The CS50P course materials identify a Creative Commons Attribution–NonCommercial–ShareAlike license for course materials. A paid Lumincode product should therefore get a rights review before using those materials as a marketed part of the paid course. Confirm the permitted treatment of embedded recordings with the relevant rights holders and review the applicable YouTube terms. This project embeds official videos and does not download, mirror, or rehost them.
